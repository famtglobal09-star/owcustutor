What is DV360?

Display & Video 360 is a demand-side platform used to plan, buy, and optimize digital media across display, video, audio, and connected TV inventory.

  • Build advertisers, campaigns, insertion orders, and line items.
  • Define audiences, budgets, targeting, and flight dates.
  • Review creative delivery and campaign reporting.
02

What is CM360?

Campaign Manager 360 is an ad serving and measurement platform used to organize creatives, placements, tags, tracking, and delivery across campaigns.

  • Set up campaigns, placements, ads, and creative assets.
  • Generate tags and verify tracking workflows.
  • Inspect delivery status and placement-level results.
